The BrannEco difference

Why Buy From BrannEco?

🌱 Truly Plastic-Free

Our areca range uses naturally fallen leaves, not cut trees. Every material is biodegradable and home-compostable in 60–90 days.

✅ Certified Food-Safe

Food-grade materials, free from heavy metals and harsh chemicals. Suitable for hot or cold food, with microwave- and oven-safe options.

🌍 Real Export Track Record

Flexible FOB/CIF terms, from trial cartons to full containers. Documentation and freight support help keep orders moving.

🎨 Custom Moulds On Demand

Bring your shape, size or logo idea. We develop the mould, share a sample for approval, then prepare for production.

💰 Transparent Pricing

See product pricing in your selected currency. Request a quote for confirmed order, shipping and destination costs.

🎓 Backed by dorm2vc

Your own shape, size or logo — embossed into areca leaf, bagasse or kraft tableware.

How Custom Moulds Work
  1. Share your design — a sketch, photo, or reference product, plus target size and material.
  2. Mould development — our team CNC-machines a custom die to your specification.
  3. Sample approval — we ship physical samples for you to check fit, finish and branding.
  4. Bulk production — once approved, we move straight to your production run (trial carton or full container).
